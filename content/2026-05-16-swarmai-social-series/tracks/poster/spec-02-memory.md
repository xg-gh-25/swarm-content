### Colors
- Background: #0D1117 (GitHub dark)
- Hero text: #FFFFFF
- Body text: #FFFFFF at 80% opacity
- Accent/tagline: #D4A853 (warm gold)
- Subtle visual element: gradient or layered shapes in #1B2838 → #0D1117

### Typography
- Hero: PingFang SC Semibold, 72px
- Body: PingFang SC Regular, 32px
- Tagline: Inter Medium, 24px
- All left-aligned, 80px left margin

### Visual concept
Abstract representation of "depth/continuity" — NOT a literal timeline or database.
Options:
- Concentric translucent layers (depth accumulation)
- Gradient fade from dark to warm (knowledge building up)
- Subtle golden ring/arc (continuity metaphor)

Keep minimal. Text is the hero. Visual is atmosphere, not illustration.

### Constraints
- No code screenshots
- No terminal UI
- No person/avatar
- No literal "brain" or "memory chip" imagery
- Abstract/geometric only
